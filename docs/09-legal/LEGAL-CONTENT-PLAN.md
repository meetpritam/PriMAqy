# Legal Content & Guardrails Plan — PriMAqy

---

## 1. Mandatory Legal Guardrail Rule

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The PriMAqy codebase and content pipeline must NEVER claim Private Limited status, LLP incorporation, DPIIT recognition, Startup Bihar recognition, patent ownership, or trademark registration unless explicitly verified in `src/data/company.ts`.

Default positioning across all legal pages: **"PriMAqy — AI & Technology Venture"**.

---

## 2. Legal Policy Suite (Module 5 Scope)

- **Privacy Policy (`/privacy`)**: Data collection, zero unauthorized tracking, cookie usage.
- **Terms of Service (`/terms`)**: Platform usage terms, intellectual property, disclaimer of warranties.
- **Cookie Policy (`/legal/cookies`)**: Explanation of essential functional cookies.
- **Community Guidelines (`/legal/community`)**: Rules for community forums and interaction.
- **Content Policy (`/legal/content`)**: Attribution, reuse, and copyright notices.
