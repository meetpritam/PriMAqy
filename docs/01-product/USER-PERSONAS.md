# User Personas — PriMAqy & Toolsetic

This document defines 10 core target personas for PriMAqy and its primary product ecosystem, Toolsetic.

---

## 1. Developer Persona
- **Role**: Software Engineer / Full-Stack Developer
- **Goals**: Discover high-performance AI utilities, review technical architecture, evaluate API capabilities.
- **Problems**: Bloated developer tooling, slow performance, complex API documentation.
- **Needs**: Clean interfaces, fast loading times, transparent technical documentation.
- **Relevant Sections**: `/technology`, `/products/toolsetic`, `/insights`.

## 2. Creator Persona
- **Role**: Digital Content Creator / Designer / Writer
- **Goals**: Streamline daily content generation, editing, and formatting tasks using AI productivity tools.
- **Problems**: Disconnected content utilities, expensive subscription paywalls.
- **Needs**: Intuitive UI, practical AI tools, fast turnaround.
- **Relevant Sections**: `/products/toolsetic`, `/community`, `/newsletter`.

## 3. Student Persona
- **Role**: Academic Student / Independent Learner
- **Goals**: Access study utilities, research assistance, and document productivity tools.
- **Problems**: High costs of academic software, steep learning curves.
- **Needs**: Free or accessible utilities, clear explanations, privacy safety.
- **Relevant Sections**: `/products/toolsetic`, `/insights`, `/about`.

## 4. Founder Persona
- **Role**: Early-Stage Startup Founder
- **Goals**: Explore practical AI workflow tools for team productivity, evaluate PriMAqy product vision.
- **Problems**: Operational overhead, limited budget for software suites.
- **Needs**: Scalable productivity solutions, clear product roadmaps.
- **Relevant Sections**: `/startup`, `/roadmap`, `/products/toolsetic`.

## 5. Small Business Owner Persona
- **Role**: Small Business Operator / Freelance Consultant
- **Goals**: Automate daily digital administrative and content creation tasks.
- **Problems**: Lack of dedicated IT support, expensive enterprise software.
- **Needs**: Reliable, simple, out-of-the-box digital tools.
- **Relevant Sections**: `/products`, `/contact`, `/about`.

## 6. Technology Enthusiast Persona
- **Role**: Tech Advocate / AI Community Member
- **Goals**: Stay informed on emerging AI trends, venture updates, and open technology.
- **Problems**: Misleading marketing claims, lack of technical transparency.
- **Needs**: Honest tech insights, active community engagement, clear roadmaps.
- **Relevant Sections**: `/insights`, `/community`, `/roadmap`, `/newsletter`.

## 7. Startup / Incubator Reviewer Persona
- **Role**: Program Manager / Evaluator
- **Goals**: Review venture identity, team composition, product brief, and startup documents.
- **Problems**: Opaque startup claims, unverified corporate metrics.
- **Needs**: Verified public document registry, clear venture location (Patna, Bihar), transparent roadmap.
- **Relevant Sections**: `/startup/documents`, `/team`, `/about`, `/legal`.

## 8. Potential Investor Persona
- **Role**: Angel Investor / Venture Partner
- **Goals**: Understand PriMAqy product vision, market positioning, and roadmap trajectory.
- **Problems**: Lack of structured investor materials and clear corporate identity.
- **Needs**: Clean investor portal, transparent milestones, clear contact paths.
- **Relevant Sections**: `/investors`, `/roadmap`, `/products/toolsetic`.

## 9. Potential Partner Persona
- **Role**: Technology Vendor / Strategic Partner
- **Goals**: Explore synergy for technical integration, co-marketing, or distribution.
- **Problems**: Unclear API protocols or venture contact channels.
- **Needs**: Direct contact channels, tech stack documentation, clear partner guidelines.
- **Relevant Sections**: `/technology`, `/contact`, `/media`.

## 10. Future Employee Persona
- **Role**: Prospective Engineer / Designer / Marketer
- **Goals**: Learn about PriMAqy culture, engineering standards, open positions, and venture mission.
- **Problems**: Generic career listings lacking technical context or clear vision.
- **Needs**: Transparent engineering standards (`CODING-STANDARDS.md`), active career portal.
- **Relevant Sections**: `/careers`, `/team`, `/about`, `/technology`.
