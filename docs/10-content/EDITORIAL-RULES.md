# Editorial Rules & Tone of Voice — PriMAqy

---

## 1. Editorial Voice Guidelines

- **Tone**: Technical, precise, professional, honest, and accessible.
- **Avoid Hype**: Never use inflated claims, unverified metrics, or superficial AI buzzwords.
- **Fact Integrity**: Always state facts clearly. If a feature or product is under development, tag it as `Coming Soon` or `In Development`.
- **Legal Compliance**: Enforce `src/data/company.ts` legal status rules across all publications.
